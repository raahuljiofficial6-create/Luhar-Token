Luhar Whitepaper

Version 1.0 - 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the official whitepaper for Luhar (LHR). Built upon the robust and efficient Binance Smart Chain (BEP-20), Luhar is designed to act as the foundational anvil for a new decentralized economy. We believe in the power of forging strong, resilient financial tools accessible to anyone, anywhere.

2. Vision & Mission

Vision: To become the gold standard of store-of-value and utility tokens on the BSC network, characterized by unwavering stability and community focus.

Mission: We forge ahead by providing a transparent, immutable, and deflationary ecosystem that rewards long-term holders and actively funds continuous development and marketing through strategic token allocation.

3. Tokenomics

The total supply is strictly capped at 1,000,000 LHR. The distribution ensures a healthy genesis liquidity pool while reserving appropriate funds for future expansion without causing severe dilution.

  • 50% - Initial Liquidity Pool (Locked)
  • 20% - Marketing & Partnerships
  • 15% - Core Development & dApp Creation
  • 10% - Community Rewards & Airdrops
  • 5% - Treasury Reserve

4. Roadmap

Our roadmap is divided into clear, actionable phases. From contract deployment and initial DEX offering on PancakeSwap to prestigious aggregators like CoinMarketCap and ultimately Tier-1 Centralized Exchanges. (See main website timeline for visual breakdown).

5. Security

The Luhar smart contract code has been written following the highest security standards avoiding re-entrancy, overflow, and underflow vulnerabilities. Contract ownership will be renounced post-launch, and baseline liquidity locked to prevent rug-pull scenarios.

Cryptocurrency trading and investment inherently involve high levels of risk. The information provided in this whitepaper does not constitute financial advice. Users should perform their own due diligence before interacting with the LHR token.

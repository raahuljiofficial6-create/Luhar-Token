LUHAR (LHR) Whitepaper

Version 2.0 - Feb 2026

1. Introduction

Luhar (LHR) is a comprehensive DeFi ecosystem built on the Binance Smart Chain, designed to integrate staking, NFT utility, and a decentralized launchpad into a seamless experience. Our goal is to forge a strong, community-driven platform that rewards long-term holders.

2. Vision

We aim to be the foundational anvil upon which new Web3 projects are built, offering a secure environment for project incubation while providing sustainable yields to LHR stakers.

3. Utility

The \(LHR\) token serves multiple functions: governance voting, staking for yield, tiered access to the Launchpad, and as the native currency for minting Forged NFTs.
